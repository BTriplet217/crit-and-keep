What the app stores

The characters, campaigns, notes, dice styles and settings you enter are saved on your device.

If you create an account, the app also stores:

  • your email address, used only to sign you in and to send password reset emails you ask for;
  • a private copy of the characters, campaigns, notes, dice styles and settings you enter, so you can use them on another device.

You can use the app without an account. Nothing leaves your device unless you sign in.

Shared campaigns

If you invite players to a campaign, or join one with an invite code, some of what you enter is shared with the other people in that campaign:

  • the name you choose to show the group;
  • the campaign's shared pages, which everyone in the campaign can read and change;
  • the characters you add to the campaign, which the Game Master can read. Other players can read them only if you switch that on.

A Game Master's own pages (notes, combat tracker, encounters and custom monsters) are never shared with players. Leaving a campaign, or the Game Master ending it, removes your characters from it.

What the app does not do

There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your data is not sold or shared with anyone for marketing. Images you put on dice stay inside your own saved copy.

Where account data is kept

Sign-in and saved copies are handled by Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which stores the data on Google's servers on our behalf. Access rules allow each signed-in person to read and change only their own saved copy, plus the campaigns they have joined as described above.

Deleting your data

In the app: open Settings, then Account, then choose "Delete account". This permanently removes your account and the copy saved to it, closes any campaigns you were sharing as Game Master, and takes you out of campaigns you had joined.

Without the app: email [CONTACT EMAIL] from the address on the account and ask for it to be deleted. Requests are handled within 30 days.

Data saved only on your device is removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and accounts are not intended for them.
